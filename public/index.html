FAQ

How do I pay?

Tap Subscribe, open the bot in Telegram and send /subscribe. Pay with Telegram Stars directly in the Telegram app.

Does it renew automatically?

Yes. The plan renews every 30 days for 99 ⭐ until you cancel. Use /cancel_subscription in the bot — you keep your perks until the current period ends.

What happens when it expires?

Photo sending switches off and the PREMIUM tag is removed. You can keep chatting with text as a free member.

How do I check my status?

Send /status to the bot to see your expiry date, remaining time and renewal status.
